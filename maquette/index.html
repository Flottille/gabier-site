SSH daemon · Rust · Discord & Slack

Know who boards your server.

Gabier watches the SSH logins on your VPS and pings you on Discord or Slack within a second. One Rust binary, zero dependencies, one line of config.

Install in 2 minutes View the code
$cargo install gabier

What you get

A clean embed you can read from your phone: who, from where, how. Mention yourself or your admin role on accepted logins, and keep quiet for your static IP or your backup account.

Small, quiet, always on watch

Gabier does one thing and does it well. No heavy agent, no dashboard to host.

Within a second

Streams journald or auth.log. The moment sshd accepts a session, the notification is on its way.

Hardened by default

Ships with a systemd unit: dynamic user, read-only system, no capabilities. It reads the journal, talks to your chat, nothing else.

You pick the noise level

Accepted logins only, or failures and disconnects too. Ignore lists for users and IPs.

Discord and Slack

A plain webhook, no bot to invite. Both at once if you like. Telegram, ntfy and email are coming.

One binary, that's it

Written in Rust, statically linked, a few MB. Debian, Ubuntu, Fedora, Arch, Alma: anywhere sshd runs.

Open source

MIT licensed. Read it, audit it, fork it. The parser is covered by tests.

How it works

01 · Watch
journalctl -f -u ssh

Gabier follows the sshd journal (or a plain log file, rotation included).

02 · Recognize
Accepted publickey for…

sshd lines are parsed: user, IP, port, method, key fingerprint.

03 · Shout
POST webhook Discord / Slack

A message lands in your Discord or Slack channel. Pince did his job; back to what you were doing.

Install in 2 minutes

Create a webhook in Discord (Channel settings → Integrations → Webhooks) or Slack (Incoming Webhooks), paste it in the config, start the service.

$ cargo install gabier
$ sudo mkdir -p /etc/gabier
$ gabier init | sudo tee /etc/gabier/config.toml # then paste your webhook
$ sudo gabier test                              # Pince waves hello
$ sudo systemctl enable --now gabier
